1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Shield-X ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, processes, stores, and, where applicable, shares information when you use its Android device-security, network-analysis, local AI, and device-maintenance features. Features and permissions vary by App version and device. We aim to request only the access necessary for the features you choose to use.

2. Information the App may access

Depending on the features you enable and the permissions you grant, the App may access:

  • Device and performance information: Android version, device model, battery state, CPU and memory utilization, storage capacity, and other device-health measurements.
  • Application information: installed app names or package identifiers, app versions, and security-relevant app details, where Android allows access and you have granted the applicable permission.
  • Network and security information: connection status, network type, IP or DNS configuration, security-test results, connection metadata, and URLs or domains you ask the App to check.
  • Files and storage information: file metadata or file contents only to the extent needed for a scan or maintenance action the App supports and Android permits.
  • Local AI inputs and results: prompts, scan findings, locally saved learning preferences, or feedback supplied to an on-device model if that feature is available.
  • Settings and operational logs: preferences, scan history, diagnostic events, and cached security definitions needed for operation.

Access to these categories does not mean every item is collected or transmitted. Some categories may constitute personal or sensitive data under applicable rules.

3. How information is used

We use information available to the App to present device-security findings, detect potential risks, assist with network checks, provide device-resource insights, support user-approved cleanup operations, operate supported on-device AI functions, retain local preferences, and troubleshoot functionality. Security alerts and automated recommendations are informational and do not guarantee detection of every threat.

Any file deletion, permission change, or other consequential action should be presented to you for review and confirmation unless the App clearly explains a separately enabled automation setting.
